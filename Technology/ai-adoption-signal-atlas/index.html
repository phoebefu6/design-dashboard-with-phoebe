A measured estimate, not a headcount

METHODOLOGY

What each dot means

Microsoft defines AI diffusion as the share of people aged 15–64 who used a generative AI product during the reporting period. The estimate is based on aggregated, anonymized telemetry and adjusted for operating-system and device-market share, internet penetration, and population.

Country AI user share×Population aged 15–64=Estimated users

Dot counts use largest-remainder rounding so the full field totals 946 dots. Twelve neutral dots represent the difference between Microsoft’s global rate and the sum of its published country estimates. This avoids inventing a country assignment.

Appropriate interpretation: directional adoption at population scale. Not appropriate: exact unique-user counts, product market share, or model preference.
