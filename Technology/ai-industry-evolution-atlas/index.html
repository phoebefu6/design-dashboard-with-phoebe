Method / sources / limits

What the flowers can—and cannot—say

Two attention lenses

Historical blooms use the frequency of “artificial intelligence” in English-language books. From 2017 onward, blooms use user pageviews for the English Wikipedia article. A visible seam marks this method change.

Local normalization

Every bloom rescales its own observations from minimum to maximum. Radius reveals the contour within an era only; it never claims that one era had more absolute interest than another.

Selected history

Spokes are an editorial selection of documented turning points, not a complete history. Filled endpoints mark breakthroughs or adoption; hollow endpoints mark setbacks, harms, backlash, or governance.

Partial latest year

2026 includes January through June only. The final pageview observation is therefore labeled 2026 H1 and should not be compared with a full calendar year.
